Locked-screen audio test

Running as:

Enter the probe token (PROBE_TOKEN in bakeoff/.env). This phone remembers it; a Home Screen app asks once more.

Keep-alive: the phone loops silence it makes itself (nothing to hear, nothing sent over the network), so iOS keeps the page running between clips.

A clip every:

Each clip is about 8 s of numbered Cantonese. Listen to the first one or two, then lock the phone with the side button for about 3 minutes (with 60 s; about 5 minutes with 2 min), with earphones in or the speaker on. Note the numbers you hear, unlock, and tap Stop. A Safari tab or the Home Screen icon both work (Share → Add to Home Screen, keep "Open as Web App" on).
